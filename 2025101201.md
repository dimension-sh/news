It's not something I planned when I started Dimension, but unfortunately we've reached the end of the line. Personal life changes mean that continuing the maintenance of the system is difficult and as we're based on a EOL operating system it is too much of a risk to keep running.

`s1.dimension.sh` will be shutting down **2025-10-15 10:00 BST**.

Any backups of your data has been purged as per [our policy](https://dimension.sh/cgi/wiki.cgi?p=Data_Retention). In your home directories you'll find a copy of your data named `<username>.tar.gz`.

On **2025-10-13** interactive logins to the system will be disabled and only allowed via SFTP/SCP to recover any files and backups.

It's been a good five years, for alternative systems check out the [Tildeverse](https://tildeverse.org/members/).
